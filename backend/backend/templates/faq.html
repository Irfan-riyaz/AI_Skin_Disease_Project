Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. This system provides AI-assisted screening and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

What image quality is required?

Use a clear, focused image of the lesion with good lighting and minimal obstructions.

How accurate is the model?

Accuracy varies by condition and image quality. The app shows a confidence score for each prediction.

Where are my images stored?

Uploaded images are stored temporarily on the server in the application `uploads/` folder for processing.
